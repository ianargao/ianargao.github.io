About me

Hi! My name is John Michael F. Argao, but my friends call me JM or Argao. My crush, however, calls me Ian—yiee! 😆

I live in M.S. Garcia, Cabanatuan City, and I am currently a BSCS student at Holy Cross College of Sta. Rosa, Nueva Ecija.

Why did I choose this program? First, I believe it is relevant all over the world because technology is everywhere. Wherever you go, you will find technology being used in different ways. And who knows? Maybe someday, I'll become the next Tony Stark. 😂

But the truth is, I chose this program without having a specific reason. I simply wanted to study, get my diploma, and, honestly, sleep at home. 😂

When we were building this portfolio — and when I say "we," I mean myself and the AI tools I use — every idea came from my own mind, while AI helped me bring them to life.

So, what is the point?

The point is that you don't have to do everything alone. We all need help from others to grow, learn from our mistakes, and turn our ideas into reality. The right people and tools can lift you higher than you ever could on your own.

And my favorite quote is:

"Failure is not the end — it is simply part of success."

That's all for my simple introduction. Thank you! ❤️
